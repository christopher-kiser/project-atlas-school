## Viewing and verification

University and publisher recordings are credited and freely linked as supplementary material. Atlas does not own these videos, award publisher credentials, or claim a partnership. Watching does not advance assessed progress. No video files were downloaded or rehosted. This personal GitHub Pages edition is freely accessible with no account or payment. Embedded media contacts its provider only after the learner loads the player. Publisher recordings remain publicly available at their original hosts.

The Harvard CS50 Lecture 0 YouTube embed reported unavailable despite a valid title and HTTP response. Its replacement streams the official Harvard MP4 and has been observed playing inside the Atlas page. A verified English transcript is provided; no native VTT caption track was found. Other YouTube selections were tested for actual playback advancement, not merely successful link responses. Microsoft networking/PowerShell and AWS architecture use their direct publisher players. HashiCorp remains a direct official Wistia tutorial; cookie preferences can affect its player.

Playback checks establish a recording started in the test browser, not that every minute was reviewed or that all devices/regions will work. Direct publisher links remain available. Runtime labels are measured where recorded; lecture labels otherwise identify the selected part of a series. Existing old-platform demonstrations are labeled as such.

## Curriculum fit

`videosForLesson` in `src/content/videos.ts` uses explicit lesson IDs, avoiding unrelated videos added solely because they share a broad section. The browser acceptance check opens the Videos stage of every released lesson and requires at least one assigned selection. Python files, Python libraries/APIs, SQL writing/transactions and PowerShell have dedicated selections. The practical prompts supply Atlas-specific application; external videos do not replace a complete guided lab.
