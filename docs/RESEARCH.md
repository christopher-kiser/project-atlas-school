# Curriculum and certification research

Research date: **22 October 2026**. This is a dated editorial review, not continuous monitoring. Certification checkout, regional pricing, exam versions, language availability, and external pages can change. The live catalogs are `src/content/resources.ts` and `src/content/certifications.ts`.

## How the sources shaped Atlas

Atlas combines the following strengths rather than reproducing one exam syllabus. The sequencing, teaching examples, readiness rubrics, and career gates are original Atlas judgments. Vendor objectives establish topic coverage; they do not establish that a learner can troubleshoot independently or will obtain a job.

Prices are budget references, not purchase quotes. Atlas does not apply promotions, assume a free retake unless explicitly stated, purchase exams, enroll the learner, or accept provider terms. It does not infer financial ROI from vendor salary marketing. Recommendations reflect curriculum fit, overlap, and opportunity cost; they should be revisited against actual vacancies and the learner’s budget.

## Readiness is independent of the vendor pass score

The catalog’s 85% threshold on two different timed practice sets separated by at least seven days is an **Atlas study recommendation**, not an official pass mark or validated predictor. It is intended to discourage memorizing repeated questions. The lab-based alternatives use an Atlas rubric in timed practice; they are not representations of the provider’s exam scoring.

Passing an Atlas exercise, watching a resource, or ticking a certification checklist does not establish that an external certification has been earned. The learner needs legitimate question sources and must review the complete current exam objectives. Sections attached to a checkpoint indicate relevant preparation; optional specialist exams explicitly require material beyond the current introductory sections.

## Link and video verification limits

Each catalog URL was checked through the research browser. The catalog records the review date; it does not imply a live link monitor. Canonical redirects found during review were incorporated, including Ubuntu Server, MDN’s HTTP guide, HashiCorp certification pages, and GitHub’s responsible-use page. The GitHub Skills landing page now redirects to GitHub Learn, so the catalog links to the specific verified introduction repository.

Cisco Networking Academy’s course URL resolves to a JavaScript application. Its full enrollment experience was not exercised. The GNU-hosted Bash reference manual was indexed but direct retrieval timed out or returned HTTP 403; the catalog instead uses the accessible Bash manual rendering on man7.org. LPI learning-material URLs returned a restriction, so Atlas links to the accessible current objective page instead. Account-required training, checkout flows, and paid labs were not tested. A second automated HTTP pass returned HTTP 200 for the other ordinary catalog pages, except Cisco exam pages (HTTP 403) and YouTube (HTTP 429); those were separately readable through the research browser. These restrictions are not represented as proof of playback or enrollment.

The selected videos have a stronger provenance chain than search-only recommendations:

- [The Shell, 2020](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z56Jmr9Z34Q) is embedded by the [official MIT lecture page](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/2020/course-shell/); the YouTube page returned its matching title.
- [Version Control and Git, 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9K8lB61dl3Y) is embedded by the [official 2026 lecture page](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/2026/version-control/); the YouTube page returned its matching title.

Playback, captions, geographic restrictions, and every minute of video content were not tested. The 2020 shell video is intentionally labeled with its year; enduring shell concepts are useful, while platform details should be checked against current documentation. A second 2026 shell video was discovered but omitted because direct retrieval was throttled. No playlist or video identifier was invented.

## Maintenance triggers

Before publishing new exam guidance, recheck the official name, objectives, booking availability, and checkout price. Before expanding a blueprint lesson, verify its command examples against the learner’s actual environment and add independent evidence requirements. Recheck links when a learner reports a failure or during a deliberate content review. Preserve source dates and distinguish editorial recommendations from vendor facts.
