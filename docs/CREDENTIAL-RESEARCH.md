## What the labels mean

- **Professional certification:** a provider’s named certification awarded after its certification assessment. A digital image of that certification is not a second credential.
- **Skills credential:** an assessed, bounded scenario or practical skill, such as Applied Skills or an AWS microcredential; its scope is narrower than a role certification.
- **Digital badge:** a verifiable learning award with issuer criteria, such as the assessed Cisco, IBM, and AWS paths below. It is not equivalent to CCNA, an AWS Certification, or an independently assessed engineering portfolio.
- **Course certificate:** evidence of course completion. No completion-only certificate was added merely to increase the catalogue size.

`Free credential` means public provider evidence supports a no-fee award route. Optional premium labs, personal cloud usage, subscriptions, instructor-led courses, retakes, or later professional exams must be checked separately. `Verify availability/price` means an important current access or price claim could not be established from the reviewed public evidence. All accounts still need to meet current provider terms, regional access, language, identity, and accessibility requirements.

## AWS paid-exam verification

CLF-C02 remains **$100 USD base price** on the [Cloud Practitioner exam page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-cloud-practitioner/). Its [current guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/cloud-practitioner-02/cloud-practitioner-02.html) weights cloud concepts 24%, security/compliance 30%, technology/services 34%, and billing/pricing/support 12%. The guide excludes implementation and troubleshooting from the target tasks. A knowledge pass therefore cannot establish cloud engineering readiness.

SAA-C03 remains **$150 USD base price** on the [Solutions Architect Associate exam page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-solutions-architect-associate/). Its [current guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/solutions-architect-associate-03/solutions-architect-associate-03.html) weights secure design 30%, resilient design 26%, high-performing design 24%, and cost optimization 20%, targeting at least a year of hands-on AWS solution design. Currency, taxes, delivery, age, and identification rules need a booking-time check under [AWS candidate policies](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/policies/before-testing/). Free preparation resources do not remove exam fees.

The twelve existing catalogue IDs remain stable. Only CLF-C02 and SAA-C03 pricing/objectives were freshly rechecked in this bounded free-first review; their dates are updated accordingly. Other legacy entries retain their earlier source-check dates and now state that preparation cost was not re-verified here.

## Atlas mapping and progression judgments

These are curriculum judgments, not provider equivalence claims or hiring-return estimates. Every pathway now has explicit lesson IDs; the seven additions and the two principal AWS exams identify additional preparation gaps. Completing an Atlas lesson cannot award an external credential.

1. Complete local networking and troubleshooting work, then optionally use Cisco Networking Basics to test breadth. Add Cisco network media, wireless, and simulator work beyond Atlas host troubleshooting.
2. Choose IBM **or** OCI Foundations for cloud concepts if useful. IBM adds its simulations; OCI requires translating Atlas AWS examples to OCI service and identity terms. Preserve Linux, Git, scripting, security, and independent explanations.
3. Use AWS Cloud Essentials as the first AWS knowledge checkpoint. Add official service breadth, pricing, and support preparation. The goal is a secure AWS design and sound operational reasoning alongside the badge.
4. Choose an optional practical branch when it matches the learner’s target: Azure management requires Azure resource administration; AWS Serverless Demonstrated requires dedicated serverless preparation. OCI Architect Associate offers a free formal architecture branch while still requiring OCI-specific learning.
5. Consider CLF-C02 only when its paid assessment serves a concrete goal. Consider SAA or CloudOps later when project evidence, remaining objectives, and target jobs justify spending. Paid exams remain optional to Atlas career gates.

Readiness recommendations require independent explanation and unfamiliar scenarios, with only provider-permitted resources during assessments. Use official practice exams, original practice tasks, and legitimate unseen questions. Exclude dumps, leaked lab tasks, memorized answer banks, fabricated badges, and claims of credentials not actually awarded.

## Evidence limits and refresh

NetAcad, Skill Builder, IBM learning sign-in, and Oracle MyLearn contain JavaScript or account-gated content. Public official indexed content and issuer badge definitions supplied the evidence above; actual regional enrollment, exam launch, identity checks, and individual account entitlement remain untested. Recheck those directly before starting. If a route changes price, access, or award rules, update its cost/status/date and this source note together; do not treat the checked date as a lifetime guarantee.
